Handover & Incident Tracking

A lightweight tool for small residential teams to organise handovers, incidents and follow-up work in one place.

A practical middle ground

Designed around residential settings with approximately 20–30 beds, where teams need regular shift handovers, incident records, follow-up work, historical searches and supervisory review.

It offers an option between spreadsheets or paper records and a large ERP or comprehensive care information system.

Core areas

  • Resident management
  • Staff management
  • Rooms and beds
  • Shift handovers
  • Resident incidents
  • General incidents
  • Incident follow-up
  • Timeline and historical records
  • Search
  • Excel and report exports
  • Users and permissions
  • Standalone Windows operation

Local-first, by choice

The system runs on a standalone Windows computer. Core data stays with the organisation, without requiring continuous uploads to an external cloud service.

This reduces unnecessary external connections, ongoing data transfers to third parties and dependence on remote services. The aim is simpler deployment and less unnecessary security exposure.

Local operation does not remove all security risks. Device security, access control and backups still need appropriate care.

A one-off licence

The current licensing direction is a one-off licence for standalone use, rather than a mandatory monthly subscription. For smaller organisations, this can reduce recurring software subscription costs.

Pricing has not yet been announced. Please get in touch for licensing enquiries.
